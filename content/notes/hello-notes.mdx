I've written longer essays on [Medium](https://savi.medium.com/) for a while. But most of my thinking doesn't arrive as an essay. It shows up as a sharp opinion about an onboarding flow, a pattern I keep seeing across AI launches, or a question I can't stop chewing on during a dog walk.

So this is the place for those. Some notes will be three sentences. Some will be a page. Some will be wrong, and I'll come back and say so.

What you'll find here:

- **Teardowns**: products I admire (or don't) and why
- **AI**: what's actually changing how products get built, and what's just a demo
- **Trends**: things I'm noticing before I've fully made sense of them

If something here sparks a thought, [tell me](https://www.linkedin.com/in/saviii/).
