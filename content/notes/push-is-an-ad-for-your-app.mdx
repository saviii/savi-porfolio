---
title: "Every push notification is an ad for your app"
publishedAt: "2026-10-02"
summary: "Treat the lock screen like prime real estate, because your users already do."
tags: ["teardown", "notifications"]
---

A push notification is the one moment your product gets to interrupt someone's life uninvited. That makes every notification an ad, and most of them are bad ads.

A quick gut check I use:

- **Would I pay to send this?** If the answer is no, it probably shouldn't go out.
- **Is it about them or about us?** "Your saved story has an update" beats "Don't miss today's top stories!"
- **Does it survive the lock screen?** Most people decide from the preview alone.

The best notification systems I've worked on sent *less*, not more, and made more money doing it.
